#  Housing Affordability & Market Crisis Analysis

A three-phase data analytics project examining the US housing affordability 
crisis across 5 major metro areas (2015–2024).

## Phase 1 — Excel Dashboard 
**Question:** How have home prices and affordability changed over the past decade?

**Key metric:** Affordability Index = Median Home Price ÷ (Annual Income × 5)
- Score of 1.0 = perfectly affordable
- Score above 1.5 = significantly unaffordable

**Key findings:**
- Los Angeles is the least affordable city, with an index above 2.0 since 2020
- Houston remained the most affordable throughout the period
- All 5 cities became less affordable after 2020

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel (data cleaning, PivotTables, line charts)

**Data sources:**
- [Zillow Research Data](https://www.zillow.com/research/data/) — ZHVI & ZORI
- [Federal Reserve FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org) — Median Household Income
